SAMPLE BANK LTD. (A PRIVATE SECTOR BANK) - FRAUD RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY

1. GOVERNANCE

1.1 The Board shall review this Fraud Risk Management Policy at least once every three years, or more frequently if the Board so decides.

1.2 The Special Committee of the Board for Monitoring and Follow-up of cases of Frauds shall consist of the Managing Director and two senior executives of the Bank, and shall be chaired by the Managing Director.

1.3 A senior official not below the rank of Deputy General Manager shall be responsible for the monitoring and reporting of frauds.

2. EARLY WARNING SIGNALS AND RED FLAGGING

2.1 The Risk Management Committee of the Board shall approve the early warning signal indicators and shall prescribe a turnaround time of not more than 30 days for examining early warning alerts.

2.2 Accounts with an aggregate exposure of Rs. 3 crore and above shall be reported in the Red Flagged Account Return on the CRILC platform within fifteen days of being red-flagged.

2.3 Once an account is red-flagged, the decision to classify it as fraud or to remove the red flag shall ordinarily be completed within 180 days from the date the account was first reported as red-flagged on the CRILC platform.

3. CLASSIFICATION AND REPORTING

3.1 Before classifying a borrower as a fraud, the Bank shall issue a show cause notice and give the borrower at least 15 days to respond.

3.2 Fraud Monitoring Returns shall be filed with the Reserve Bank for individual fraud cases involving Rs. 1 lakh and above, within 14 days of classification as fraud.

3.3 Frauds involving Rs. 1 crore and above shall be reported to the State or Union Territory Police.

3.4 Reported fraud cases involving up to Rs. 25 lakh may be closed for limited statistical and reporting purposes, where staff accountability has been examined and the investigation or trial has been pending for more than three years.

4. OTHER MEASURES

4.1 The title deeds and other title documents for all credit facilities of Rs. 5 crore and above shall be subjected to legal audit and re-verification.

4.2 An employee who reports a fraud that is later confirmed shall receive a reward of 1 per cent of the amount recovered, subject to a maximum of Rs. 1 lakh.

4.3 Members of the Fraud Monitoring Cell shall be allotted reserved parking at the head office.
