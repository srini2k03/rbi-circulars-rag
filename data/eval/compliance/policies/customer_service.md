# Sample Bank Ltd. - Customer Service and Grievance Redress Policy

## 1. Internal Ombudsman

1.1 The Internal Ombudsman shall be appointed on contract for a fixed term of three years, which may be extended once by up to two years.

1.2 The Internal Ombudsman may be removed before the completion of the contracted term with the approval of the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer.

1.3 The Bank shall submit a quarterly report on the functioning of the Internal Ombudsman to the Reserve Bank on or before the 10th day of the month following the quarter.

## 2. Complaint Handling

2.1 Complaints that are partially resolved or wholly rejected by the Bank's internal grievance redress mechanism shall be referred to the Internal Ombudsman if the complainant so requests.

2.2 The final decision on a complaint shall be communicated to the complainant within 30 days from the date on which the Bank receives the complaint.

## 3. Deceased Customers and Nomination

3.1 Claims in respect of deposit accounts of a deceased customer shall be settled within 30 days from the date of receipt of all the required documents.

3.2 Where a delay in settling a deceased customer's deposit claim is attributable to the Bank, the Bank shall pay compensation by way of interest at the Bank Rate plus 4 per cent per annum on the amount due, for the period of delay.

3.3 When settling a claim in favour of a nominee, the Bank shall obtain a bond of indemnity from the nominee where the balance in the deceased customer's account exceeds Rs. 15 lakh.

3.4 The Bank shall acknowledge the registration, cancellation or variation of a nomination within three working days, where the customer asks for an acknowledgement.

## 4. Loans and Recovery

4.1 Where the release of property documents after full repayment of a loan is delayed beyond 30 days for reasons attributable to the Bank, the Bank shall compensate the borrower at Rs. 5,000 for each day of delay.

4.2 Calls made by recovery agents to customers shall be recorded, with the customer informed that the call is being recorded, and the recordings shall be preserved for three months.

## 5. General

5.1 Complaints received through the Bank's official social media handles shall be acknowledged within four hours.

5.2 Annual Customer Service Excellence awards for employees shall be decided by the Head of Human Resources.
