# Sample Bank Ltd. - Retail Products Policy (Cards and Deposits)

## 1. Credit Cards

1.1 Where a credit card has not been activated by the customer within 30 days of issue, the Bank shall seek One Time Password based consent from the cardholder for activating it.

1.2 A request to close a credit card shall be completed within seven working days, subject to payment of all dues; for any delay, the Bank shall pay the cardholder Rs. 500 for each working day of delay.

1.3 A credit card account shall be reported as past due to credit information companies, and late payment charges levied, only when the account remains past due for more than three days.

1.4 Renewed credit cards shall be dispatched only after obtaining the cardholder's explicit prior consent.

1.5 Recurring deposit accounts with three consecutive missed monthly instalments shall be closed and the balance transferred to the depositor's savings account.

## 2. Debit Cards

2.1 Debit cards may be issued on savings bank accounts, current accounts and cash credit accounts.

## 3. Deposits

3.1 Differential interest rates may be offered on bulk deposits, that is, single rupee term deposits of Rs. 2 crore and above.

3.2 Interest on savings deposits shall be credited at half-yearly intervals.

3.3 Interest rates payable on bulk deposits shall be disclosed in advance on the Bank's website.

3.4 Unclaimed proceeds of a matured term deposit shall earn interest at the savings account rate or the contracted rate on the matured deposit, whichever is lower.

3.5 Requests to activate an inoperative account shall be processed within three working days of receipt of the complete application.

## 4. General

4.1 The Cards business unit shall celebrate its foundation day on 1 July each year.
