SAMPLE BANK LTD. - INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY AND CYBER SECURITY POLICY

1. IT GOVERNANCE

1.1 The Board-level IT Strategy Committee shall have at least three directors as members and shall be chaired by an independent director with a minimum of seven years' experience in managing or guiding information technology initiatives.

1.2 The Chief Information Security Officer shall report to the Head of IT and shall be given targets for the timely delivery of IT projects.

1.3 The IT, information security and cyber security policies shall be reviewed by the Board once every two years.

2. SECURITY OPERATIONS

2.1 Vulnerability assessment of critical information systems and of systems in the DMZ with a customer interface shall be conducted at least once every six months, and penetration testing at least once every twelve months.

2.2 Disaster recovery drills for critical information systems shall be conducted at least once a year.

2.3 Multi-factor authentication shall be implemented for privileged users of critical information systems where the Bank's risk assessment considers it necessary.

3. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT

3.1 Cyber incidents shall be reported to the Reserve Bank of India within six hours of detection.

3.2 Service providers shall report cyber incidents to the Bank within 24 hours of detection, and the Bank shall report them to the Reserve Bank of India within six hours of receiving the report.

4. DIGITAL PAYMENTS

4.1 All digital payment transactions shall be reconciled with every counterparty not later than 24 hours from receipt of the settlement files.

4.2 Card-not-present digital payment transactions shall be authenticated by at least two factors, one of which may be a static password.

4.3 The mobile banking application shall be bound to the customer's device using the device's hardware identifier alone.

4.4 The Bank shall migrate all customer-facing systems to quantum-safe cryptographic algorithms by 31 March 2029.

5. GENERAL

5.1 The IT Department's annual planning offsite shall be held in the fourth quarter of each financial year.
