# Sample Bank Ltd. - Know Your Customer and Anti-Money Laundering Policy

## 1. Policy and Governance

1.1 This Policy, comprising the Customer Acceptance Policy, Risk Management, Customer Identification Procedures and Monitoring of Transactions, shall be approved by the Board of Directors or by a committee to which the Board has delegated this power.

1.2 The money laundering and terrorist financing risk assessment shall be carried out at a periodicity decided by the Board, and in any case at least once every two years.

## 2. Customer Acceptance

2.1 A branch may reject an application for onboarding or periodic updation of KYC where the customer does not furnish the documents within the stipulated time.

## 3. Customer Due Diligence

3.1 For a company, the beneficial owner shall be the natural person who, alone or together, owns or is entitled to more than 25 per cent of the shares, capital or profits of the company, or who exercises control.

3.2 Accounts opened in non-face-to-face mode using Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC shall have an aggregate deposit balance of not more than Rs. 1 lakh and aggregate credits of not more than Rs. 2 lakh in a financial year, and term loans sanctioned in such accounts shall not exceed Rs. 50,000 in a year.

3.3 Accounts opened through the Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) shall be made operational only after the account has been subjected to concurrent audit.

## 4. Ongoing Due Diligence

4.1 The risk categorisation of customer accounts shall be reviewed at the time of periodic updation of KYC.

4.2 Periodic updation of KYC shall be carried out at least once every two years for high-risk customers, once every eight years for medium-risk customers and once every ten years for low-risk customers, from the date of opening of the account or the last KYC updation.

4.3 Before the due date for periodic updation of KYC, the Bank shall send the customer at least three advance intimations through the available communication channels.

4.4 Where an existing customer, or the beneficial owner of an existing account, subsequently becomes a politically exposed person, the business relationship may continue with the approval of the Branch Manager.

## 5. Record Management and Reporting

5.1 Records of transactions shall be preserved for at least ten years from the date of the transaction, and records of customer identification and address for at least five years after the business relationship has ended.

5.2 The KYC records of new customers shall be uploaded to the Central KYC Records Registry within 30 days of the commencement of the account-based relationship.

5.3 All customers shall be screened against adverse media sources at onboarding and at least once a year thereafter.

## 6. Administration

6.1 The Compliance Department shall hold its weekly coordination meeting every Monday at 10 a.m.
