SAMPLE BANK LTD. - DIGITAL LENDING POLICY

1. LOAN TERMS

1.1 Borrowers shall be given the option to exit a digital loan during a cooling-off period of three days from disbursal, by paying the principal and the proportionate annual percentage rate without any penalty.

1.2 The credit limit of a digital borrower may be increased automatically where the repayment record is satisfactory, after informing the borrower by SMS.

2. DISBURSEMENT AND CHARGES

2.1 Digital loans may be disbursed to the account of the lending service provider for onward credit to the borrower.

2.2 Fees and charges that are not mentioned in the Key Facts Statement shall not be charged to the borrower.

3. BORROWER PROTECTION

3.1 If a complaint against the Bank or its lending service provider is not resolved within 30 days, the borrower may lodge a complaint under the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.

3.2 Before a recovery agent contacts a defaulting borrower, the particulars of the agent shall be communicated to the borrower by email or SMS.

3.3 The Bank's digital lending application may access the borrower's contact list, with the borrower's explicit consent, for verifying references.

4. DATA STORAGE

4.1 All data collected through the Bank's digital lending applications shall be stored only on servers located in India.

5. DEFAULT LOSS GUARANTEE

5.1 The Bank may accept default loss guarantee arrangements from lending service providers on credit card portfolios sourced digitally, with cover not exceeding 5 per cent of the amount disbursed.

5.2 The Bank shall invoke a default loss guarantee within a maximum overdue period of 120 days, unless the borrower makes good the dues earlier.

6. GENERAL

6.1 Machine-learning credit-scoring models used for digital loans shall undergo an independent bias audit every year.

6.2 Prizes for the Digital Lending team's quarterly internal hackathon shall be approved by the Head of Innovation.
