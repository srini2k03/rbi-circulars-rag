# Sample Bank Ltd. - Outsourcing of Financial and IT Services Policy

## 1. Scope and Governance

1.1 The Bank may outsource financial services such as cash-handling support, but it shall not outsource core management functions, namely internal audit, the compliance function, decisions on compliance with KYC norms for opening deposit accounts, sanction of loans (including retail loans) and management of the investment portfolio.

1.2 The internal audit function may be outsourced to a reputed firm of chartered accountants with the approval of the Audit Committee of the Board.

1.3 The Bank shall maintain a central record of all material outsourcing of financial services, which shall be updated promptly and reviewed by the Board once a year.

## 2. Selection of Service Providers

2.1 A service provider of financial services, if not a subsidiary of the Bank, shall not be owned or controlled by any director of the Bank or by a relative of a director.

2.2 Outsourcing contracts shall not run for more than five years, after which the activity shall be re-tendered.

## 3. Outsourcing Agreements

3.1 Every outsourcing agreement shall provide for the right of the Reserve Bank of India to access the Bank's records held by the service provider and to cause an inspection of the service provider.

3.2 A service provider may engage subcontractors for any part of the outsourced activity after informing the Bank in writing.

3.3 In the event of a breach of security or leakage of confidential customer information at a service provider, the Bank shall notify the Reserve Bank of India within thirty days.

## 4. Monitoring and Termination

4.1 The Bank shall review the financial and operational condition of each provider of outsourced financial services at least once every two years.

4.2 On terminating the services of a service provider, the Bank shall publicise the termination so that its customers do not continue to deal with the service provider.

4.3 Where a financial service is outsourced to an offshore service provider, all original records shall continue to be maintained in India.

## 5. Administration

5.1 The Procurement Department shall use the Bank's standard letterhead and branding in all correspondence with prospective vendors.
