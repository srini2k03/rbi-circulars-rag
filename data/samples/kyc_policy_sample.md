# Sample Bank Ltd. - Know Your Customer (KYC) and AML Policy

Version 3.2. Approved by the Board of Directors on 14 March 2025. This sample is a
synthetic document written for demonstrating the compliance checker; it is not the
policy of any real bank.

## 1. Scope and Applicability

1.1 This Policy applies to all branches, business units and overseas offices of the Bank
and to all customers, including walk-in customers.

1.2 The Policy shall be reviewed by the Board at least once every two years.

## 2. Customer Acceptance Policy

2.1 The Bank shall not open an account in anonymous or fictitious (benami) names.

2.2 The Bank shall not open an account where it is unable to apply appropriate Customer
Due Diligence (CDD) measures, either due to non-cooperation of the customer or
non-reliability of the documents furnished by the customer.

## 3. Customer Due Diligence

3.1 For individuals, the Bank shall obtain the following:
(a) the Aadhaar number, where the customer desires to receive any benefit or subsidy
under any scheme notified under section 7 of the Aadhaar Act, or proof of possession of
Aadhaar number;
(b) the Permanent Account Number or Form No. 60; and
(c) such other documents as the Bank may require.

3.2 Periodic updation of KYC shall be carried out at least once every three years for
high risk customers, once every eight years for medium risk customers and once every
ten years for low risk customers.

## 4. Record Management

4.1 The Bank shall maintain all necessary records of transactions between the Bank and
the customer for at least three years from the date of the transaction.

## 5. Staff Matters

5.1 Branch staff are entitled to casual leave of twelve days in a calendar year, as per
the Bank's HR Manual.
